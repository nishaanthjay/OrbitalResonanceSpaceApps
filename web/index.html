Known limits

  • An earlier window (Rondonia) had no alert-confirmed clearing of 1 ha or more in its usable dates (2026-08-29 to 2026-10-16), so this window was chosen instead. Results describe this window only.
  • Rain and soil moisture can cause false alarms
  • Fire double-bounce can mimic or mask clearing
  • Flooded forest causes false alarms
  • Selective logging is not detected
  • Tested on one area only
